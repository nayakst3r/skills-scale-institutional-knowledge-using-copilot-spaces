# FAR accountability statement — draft

**Accountable person:** [name]
**Position:** Ontology Product Owner, [division]
**Registered with:** APRA and ASIC under the Financial Accountability Regime

## Part of the business for which accountable

The enterprise semantic layer: the ontology, its automated maintenance pipeline,
the MCP servers that serve it, and the data products that consume it.

## Key functions

- End-to-end accountability for the accuracy and currency of the ontology as a
  production data asset.
- Accountability for the design and operation of the automated maintenance
  pipeline, including the boundary between automated and human-approved change.
- Accountability for the regulatory obligation mapping held within the ontology.

## Reasonable steps

1. Maintain a board-approved change-class policy defining what may be automated.
2. Maintain the immutable audit log and ensure segregation of duties between the
   proposing agent and the approving human.
3. Maintain tolerance levels for ontology availability and accuracy, and test them.
4. Ensure the platform vendor is assessed and, if material, registered under CPS 230.
5. Ensure independent assurance at least annually.
6. Escalate to the Board Risk Committee where a change materially affects
   regulatory reporting or consumer outcomes.

## Interfaces

- Chief Data Officer — reporting line
- Chief Risk Officer — 2LoD challenge
- Head of Internal Audit — 3LoD assurance
- Accountable persons for regulatory reporting, technology, and each consuming domain
