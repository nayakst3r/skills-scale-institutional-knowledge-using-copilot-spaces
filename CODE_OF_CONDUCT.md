# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, together with the following project-specific expectations.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and make your own intent clear.
- Be honest about how work was produced, including AI assistance, and about what you have and have not verified.
- Give reviewers what they need: small changes, clear descriptions and evidence of testing.
- Respect other people's in-flight work. Coordinate before changing code someone else is actively working on.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or exclusionary language.
- Submitting work you have not reviewed yourself and leaving others to find its problems.
- Misrepresenting authorship or verification of a contribution.
- Pressuring reviewers to approve, or circumventing review and branch protections.

## Enforcement
Report concerns privately to the project maintainers listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`, or through your organisation's
standard conduct and ethics channels. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and may restrict access, in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
